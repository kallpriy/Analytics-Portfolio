# Sales Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Domain
Ecommerce / Retail Analytics

---

## Overview
This dashboard provides a consolidated view of ecommerce sales performance, focusing on how revenue, profit, returns, and product performance vary across regions and over time.  
The goal is not just to track numbers, but to understand **where performance is coming from and where risks may exist**, especially in terms of returns and profit concentration.

---

## Business Objective
- Track revenue, profit, and transaction volume for the current period  
- Identify products and brands that contribute most to revenue and profit  
- Monitor return rates and understand their impact on overall profitability  
- Compare regional sales contribution to highlight priority markets  
- Analyze revenue trends to identify growth and seasonal patterns  

---

## Dataset
- Ecommerce transactional data  
- Includes orders, revenue, profit, returns, products, regions, and dates  

---

## Key Metrics
- Total Revenue  
- Total Profit  
- Total Transactions  
- Return Rate  
- Product-wise Profit Margin  
- Region-wise Sales Contribution  
- Monthly and Weekly Revenue Trends  

---

## Key Insights
- Revenue and profit are driven by a relatively small group of high-performing products, indicating performance concentration rather than evenly distributed sales.  
- Some products show strong margins but also higher return rates, which can reduce net profitability if not monitored closely.  
- Sales are concentrated in specific regions, highlighting markets that contribute most to overall performance.  
- Revenue trends show steady growth with visible seasonal variation, which can be useful for planning and forecasting.  

---

## Tools Used
- Power BI  
- DAX  
- Data Modeling  

---

## Outcome
- Created a single-page performance view that combines revenue, profit, returns, and regional analysis  
- Highlighted key products and regions driving overall results  
- Enabled quick identification of profit risks related to returns and product performance
