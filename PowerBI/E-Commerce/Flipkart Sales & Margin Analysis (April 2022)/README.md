# Flipkart Sales & Margin Analysis (April 2022)

## 📌 Domain
Ecommerce / FMCG Retail Analytics

---

## Overview
This project analyzes marketplace sales performance for a single month (April 2022) with a focus on **how sales, discounts, and margins interact** at a product and category level.  
Instead of looking only at revenue, the dashboard helps understand **where discounts help drive value and where they erode margins**, which is especially important in high-volume ecommerce environments.

---

## Business Objective
- Track sales and order performance for the month of April 2022  
- Understand the relationship between **discounting and margins**  
- Identify top-performing products, brands, and categories  
- Analyze city-wise order contribution to spot demand concentration  
- Monitor daily sales patterns within a short time window  

---

## Dataset
- Source: Public ecommerce sales dataset (Kaggle)  
- Time Period: April 2022  
- Includes: Orders, net revenue, discounts, margins, products, brands, and cities  

---

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales  
- Net Revenue  
- Total Orders  
- Average Order Value  
- Average Discount (%)  
- Average Margin (%)  
- Total Products  
- Total Brands  

---

## Analysis Performed
- Analyzed daily net revenue trends to understand short-term sales movement  
- Compared product and category-level performance to identify concentration drivers  
- Reviewed brand-level demand to identify high-volume contributors  
- Analyzed city-wise order distribution to understand regional demand patterns  
- Examined the relationship between discounts and margins to highlight profitability risks  

---

## Key Insights
- Sales are concentrated among a relatively small set of products and brands, indicating dependency on a limited portfolio for volume.  
- Higher discounts do not consistently result in better margins, showing that aggressive discounting can reduce profitability despite higher sales.  
- A few cities contribute a large share of total orders, highlighting demand concentration rather than evenly distributed growth.  
- Categories with controlled discounting show more stable margins compared to heavily discounted categories.  
- Even within a single month, daily sales show noticeable fluctuations, emphasizing the importance of short-term performance tracking.
